### About

Senior Mobile Engineer specializing in React Native and TypeScript, with experience designing, building, and scaling mobile applications across fintech, banking, e-commerce, telecom, and digital platforms.

I work across the entire software development lifecycle, from technical architecture and implementation to testing, performance optimization, observability, CI/CD, and production releases.

My experience includes:

- Designing scalable mobile architectures using MVVM and modular principles.
- Building complex financial features, including Pix, payments, billing, account opening, and investment flows.
- Developing cross-platform applications with React Native, TypeScript, and Expo.
- Integrating native iOS and Android capabilities through Swift and native modules.
- Implementing secure authentication and authorization flows with Auth0.
- Improving application reliability through automated testing, monitoring, and performance optimization.
- Collaborating with product managers, designers, backend engineers, and cross-functional teams.

I also have experience as a Scrum Master, connecting technical decisions with product goals and business requirements.
